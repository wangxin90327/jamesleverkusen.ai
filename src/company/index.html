Company

Established for the long horizon

Built for the infrastructure age of intelligence.

James Leverkusen was founded in 2019 on a simple conviction: as artificial intelligence evolves from experimentation into essential infrastructure, enduring value will be created by teams that turn technical depth into practical, measurable solutions.

Since its founding, the company has completed two rounds of private financing and established a dedicated AI computing facility to support model research, data processing, technical evaluation, and the development of applied AI solutions.

Today, James Leverkusen combines long-term capital, dedicated computing capabilities, and industry expertise to support the next generation of technology companies.

Company structure

James Leverkusen is a privately held technology investment company headquartered in Los Angeles. We invest long-term capital in artificial intelligence, technology infrastructure, and enterprise software. We do not raise capital from the public or provide retail investment services.
